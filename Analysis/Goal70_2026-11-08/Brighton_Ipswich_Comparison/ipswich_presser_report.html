O'Neil: Bournemouth Are in a Good Moment
Friday, 6th Nov 2026 18:28

Town manager Gary O’Neil says his side will be facing an AFC Bournemouth team in a good moment at Portman Road on Sunday, but that doesn’t mean the Blues won’t be able to hurt them.

After a slow start to the season under new head coach Marco Rose, during which time they failed to win any of their first six Premier League games, the Cherries, who finished sixth in the division last season, have hit a rich vein of form.

They are currently on a run of seven wins in a row in all competitions, three in the Premier League, three in the Europa League and one in the Carabao Cup.

Prior to Thursday’s 3-1 victory away against 10-man Slavia Prague, they had scored 16 goals in six matches without conceding.

Bournemouth, where O’Neil cut his teeth as a manager in the 2022/23 campaign, are ninth in the table, a point ahead of the Blues, with an away record of won one, 3-0 at Manchester United a fortnight ago, drawn one, 2-2 at Newcastle in September, and lost two, 2-1 at Manchester City on the opening day and 5-1 at Chelsea in early October in the final game before their current run.

“It sounds like a difficult challenge,” O’Neil said when the Cherries’ recent record was put to him. “I think they’ve obviously found a good way at the moment. They have excellent players. 

“I watched the game last night, which was only a game for a bit. Obviously, the sending-off made a big difference to it.

“The amount of top players they’ve been able to recruit over the years that they’ve been in the Premier League, the recruitment that the whole team there has put together has been impressive.

“They are an established Premier League team now, there’s no doubt about that, you see the amount of changes they were able to make last night and still name a really strong team.

“So it’s going to be a test for us. The new coach has had a good impact and clean sheets are not easy to come by at this level, so for them to have put so many of them together in Europe and in the Premier League is impressive.

“But, as you guys know already, our focus is us. We prep ourselves for what we think we might face, but we have a good understanding of ourselves and what we need to do, so that’ll be the focus this weekend. 

“Get to Portman Road on Sunday, try and perform in a way that connects with the fans, try and create a really good atmosphere, try and show quality and see if we can improve our points tally.”

O’Neil believes the team under new boss Rose has some similarities to former head coach Andoni Ireola’s side but also varies in a number of ways.

“Pretty different,” he reflected. “Out of possession, some differences. With the ball, still pretty direct. Lots of long passes from the sort of right centre-back area, trying to get to Evanilson and Rayan directly. So, there are some similarities there. Similar rotations as well in possession.

“But I suppose out of possession is probably the biggest change. Slightly different.
But what he has, similar to the benefit I have of taking over this group, is that he has a great group there.

“Physically, they’ve run a lot, same as us. Culturally, they’re a good group. Similar to us and I think any sort of tactical tweaks that you make are easier then. When you have a group that is willing to run and is willing to work and sticks together, it makes making little tweaks tactically much, much more straightforward.

“So, there are some differences, but not radical ones. I think they’re still continuing the good work that was done by Andoni Iraola.

“But Marco has done an excellent job. It’s not easy, as you’ve seen at other clubs, it’s not easy to come in and follow on from a successful coach. Not easy. So has done very, very well, an exceptional start.”

O’Neil says they deserved more from their early season winless run rather than there being a major transformation in their form going into the current hot streak.

“They were unlucky at City, to be fair,” he reflected.” I’ve watched bits of all of their games. Even the home game with Everton [1-1 draw], they could easily have won.

“I think they were probably unfortunate to be where they were after a few games and now things, as they do, have started to go more in their favour.

“Like, last night, for instance. I thought the game was fairly balanced and then a penalty decision and a sending-off. Things can swing in your favour or against you really, really quickly.

“So, they’re in a good moment, but that doesn’t mean that we can’t hurt them. We can definitely find ways to hurt them, we can definitely find ways to make it difficult for them and we spend this week and tomorrow making sure that we’re ready to do that.”

The Team

O’Neil once again probably won’t look to make many changes with Christian Walton likely to continue in goal behind a backline of Abdoul Ouattara, Issa Diop, skipper Dara O’Shea and Leif Davis.

In midfield, Sasa Lukic seems set to return after his groin injury alongside Exequiel Palacios with Florentino Luis dropping to the bench.

Ahead of them, Abdul Fatawu will probably be on the right, Julio Enciso in the middle and one of Jaden Philogene, Jack Clarke or Daizen Maeda on the left. The Japan international may get the nod on this occasion. Emersonn looks likely to continue as the number nine.”

The Opposition

England midfielder Alex Scott has been out of action since the end of September with a thigh problem, but the 23-year-old may well be back in the squad facing the Blues.

Fellow midfielder Alex Toth is set for an extended spell out having undergone surgery on a hamstring issue.

Right-back Juanlu Sanchez is also unavailable having damaged ankle ligaments while playing for the U21s.

Central defender Veljko Milosavljevic has been out with a quad injury, while right-back Julian Araujo has also been on the sidelines with a thigh problem.

History

Overall, Town have won 15 (11 in the league) games between the sides, the Cherries 12 (12), while 12 (10) have been draws.

The teams last met at Dean Court in April 2025 when Nathan Broadhead’s first Premier League goal and Liam Delap’s 11th of the season saw the relegation-bound Blues to a 2-1 victory over AFC Bournemouth, reducing the gap to safety to nine points.

Broadhead gave Town the lead after good work from Enciso, who was on loan from Brighton, in the 34th minute, Delap smashed in the second on the hour, before Evanilson pulled one back for the Cherries, but Town held on to claim their first Premier League win of 2025.

In the preceding December at Portman Road, late goals from subs Enes Unal and Dango Ouattara saw Bournemouth come from behind to claim a 2-1 victory over the Blues in the first ever top-flight meeting between the teams.

Conor Chaplin gave Town the lead with his first Premier League goal on 21 to put the Blues in front, but Unal levelled on 87 and Ouattara grabbed the winner five minutes into injury time.

Familiar Faces

Blues boss O’Neil joined Bournemouth as a first-team coach in February 2021, then took over as head coach, initially on an interim basis, in August 2022.

Having been given the role permanently in the November, he was sacked in the summer, despite having finished 15th in their first season back in the Premier League.

O’Neil’s assistant manager Tim Jenkins was also in the same role working with him with the Cherries between December 2022 and June 2023.

Town left-back Davis was on loan at Dean Court from Leeds in the 2021/22 season, making 10 starts and five sub appearances in all competitions as Bournemouth returned to the top flight after finishing second in the Championship.

Bournemouth U21s coach Alan Connell was an U18s striker with the Blues in the early 2000s, joining the Cherries as a player following his release.

Officials

Sunday’s referee is Simon Hooper, his assistants Edward Smart and Alistair Nelson, and the fourth official Farai Hallam. The VAR is Peter Bankes and his assistant James Bell.

Wiltshire-based Hooper has shown 14 yellow cards and one red in six games so far this season.

His most recent Town match was the 1-0 defeat at Crystal Palace in March 2025 in which he yellow-carded Enciso, Jacob Greaves, Kalvin Phillips and four home players.

Hooper was also the man in black for the 2-1 win away against Wolves, then managed by Blues boss O’Neil, in the preceding December when he booked Harry Clarke and Jack Taylor during the 90 minutes, and Delap in a melee after the final whistle which saw Rayan Ait-Nouri red-carded and Matheus Cunha later charged for attacking a Town security guard, leading to a two-match ban and £80,000 fine.

Prior to that, he was in the middle for the 2-0 victory over Huddersfield Town at Portman Road in May 2024 which confirmed promotion back to the Premier League after a gap of 22 years. Omari Hutchinson and one Terrier were yellow-carded during that match.

Hooper hadn’t refereed the Blues for six years before that with his last visit to Suffolk the 4-0 defeat to Aston Villa in April 2018, the Blues’ heaviest home loss in seven years.

Town, with Bryan Klug and assistants Gerard Nash and Chris Hogg in caretaker charge, were reduced to 10 men in the 42nd minute when Grant Ward was dismissed for catching Neil Taylor with a high boot as he looked to take the ball down on the turn. Hooper showed no other cards that afternoon.

A few months earlier, in the January, he had refereed the 1-0 defeat to Wolves at Portman Road in which he booked Callum Connolly, Stephen Gleeson, Martyn Waghorn and one of the visitors.

He also refereed the 2-1 win at Burton Albion in October 2017 when he booked Luke Chambers, Cole Skuse and Bersant Celina - for his shirtless goal celebration - as well as one Brewer.

Hooper previously took charge of the previous month’s 3-1 home defeat to Bristol City in which he yellow-carded Jonas Knudsen, Joe Garner and one Robins player.

Prior to that he was the man in the middle of the 1-1 draw at home to Preston in January 2016 when he showed yellow cards to Brett Pitman and three Lilywhites.

In the preceding September he was the man in the middle during the 3-0 Capital One Cup defeat at Manchester United in which he booked only Jonny Parr.

Before that he refereed the Blues’ 2-0 home victory over Burnley at Portman Road in August of the same year in which he yellow-carded visitors’ winger Michael Kightly and no one else.

Hooper officiated in the 1-0 defeat at Reading in August 2014 in which he booked Tommy Smith, Tyrone Mings, Christophe Berra and two home players.

He also took control of the 1-0 win at Yeovil the previous season and the 3-0 home win against Crystal Palace in April 2013.

Squad From

Walton, Scherpen, O’Shea (c), Furlong, Diop, Greaves, Kipre, Davis, Ouattara, Lukic, Florentino, Palacios, Nunez, Matusiwa, Taylor, Fatawu, McAteer, Enciso, Walle Egeli, Clarke, Mehmeti, Maeda, Philogene, Emersonn, Flemming, Akpom.

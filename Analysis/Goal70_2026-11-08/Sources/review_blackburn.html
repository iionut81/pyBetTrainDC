      Blackburn 2-1 West Ham: Andri Gudjohnsen and Dapo Afolayan net to see off Hammers

      Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Blackburn and West Ham at Ewood Park on Saturday; Andri Gudjohnsen and Dapo Afolayan net in victory as Scott Parker stays winless with Hammers.

      Saturday 7 November 2026 18:42, UK

      Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

      •

      Scott Parker is still waiting for his first win as West Ham boss after suffering a 2-1 defeat at spirited Blackburn.

      It was a painful return to East Lancashire for the former Burnley boss whose side dominated possession but ultimately paid for profligacy early in the second half.

      Andri Gudjohnsen headed in his 10th league goal of the season and Dapo Afolayan's powerful strike doubled the Rovers advantage 19 minutes from the end.

      Joel Piroe lashed in from close range to halve the deficit and Tomas Soucek missed a glorious injury time chance as the Hammers suffered their first away defeat of the campaign.

      Blackburn impressively ended their two-game losing streak to move within three points of the play-offs.

      Despite West Ham's early possession, the hosts created the first real chance in the 16th minute when Jayden Fevrier's near-post cross was directed just wide by Gudjohnsen.

      West Ham raided down the left moments later and a teasing low cross from Ezra Mayers was begging to be converted but Piroe could not reach to apply the finish.

      The Hammers finally tested Balazs Toth in the 20th minute when Divine Mukasa found space on the edge of the area but the Hungarian stopper parried his shot behind.

      Konstantinos Mavropanos almost struck within a minute of the restart but glanced a towering header just wide after meeting Arne Engels' corner.

      Blackburn's goal was living a charmed life. Hammers skipper Jarrod Bowen met Edvin Austbo's centre but, though his deft header beat Toth, it cannoned back off the post.

      Parker's men kept the ball and Engels unleashed a venomous 25-yard strike that fell agonisingly wide.

      West Ham paid the price in the 54th minute when Blackburn broke up the right wing through Fevrier who delivered a pinpoint cross for the diving Gudjohnsen to head in emphatically from six yards.

      The Hammers continuted to dominate possession but with something to defend, Rovers managed more comfortably and turned the screw in the 71st minute through former Hammer Afolayan, who found space on the edge of the area and rifled a left-foot drive into the bottom right corner of Mads Hermansen's goal.

      The stunned visitors grabbed a lifeline with eight minutes remaining when Taty Castellanos' pass found its way to Piroe 12 yards out and he controlled before blasting into the roof of the net.

      Soucek should have grabbed an equaliser deep into injury time but the substitute headed straight at Toth from close range.

      The managers

      Blackburn's Tony Mowbray:

      "Today, the work rate of the team, the commitment and the endeavour, it was positive for us. Our lads rose to the challenge against a very good team.

      "We're delighted. That's a tough game. I can feel their quality, their power and their strength.

      "We scored at good times. I thought we always carried a threat. Whilst they had the ball a fair bit, I think it's not wise to go and chase and hunt down good football teams who can pick the right pass and get through you.

      "I thought we were pretty disciplined today. We had some counter attacking threat. I was happy enough at half-time but said 'let's be a bit braver, let's make sure they feel our quality', otherwise you never get them off your back."

      West Ham's Scott Parker:

      "Very disappointing. I thought we dominated first half, without really creating too much. Second half, came out really sharp, really bright and didn't manage to score the first goal.

      "Their first goal gives us a bit of a hill to climb. The second goal is obviously a foul.

      "We're bitterly disappointed because the result hasn't gone our way. There are certain things that were big improvements. I've had one training session, 45 minutes yesterday. I see a committed group at this moment in time.

      "A lot of work to be done but I'm looking forward to it."

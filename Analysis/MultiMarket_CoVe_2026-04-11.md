## 🎓 KEY LESSONS FOR SPORT ANALYST DEVELOPMENT

### 1. Edge vs Probability
- **Charlton U4.5** has 92% probability but only +1.9% edge (odds 1.11)
- **Burgos U11.5** has 95% probability AND +13.3% edge (odds 1.22)
- **Same probability range, completely different value.** Always check both.

### 2. Market Inefficiency
- Corners markets are priced less efficiently than match result or goals markets
- Segunda Division corners Under is especially mispriced — bookmakers don't model league-specific corner patterns as precisely as our Negative Binomial model does

### 3. Triple Signal Convergence
- When 3 independent models agree (DC + Goals + Corners on Torino), confidence is much higher than any single model
- This is the statistical equivalent of "triangulation" — multiple data sources confirming the same conclusion

### 4. Diversification in Picks
- Top 2 picks are from **different markets** (Corners + DC) and **different leagues** (SP2 + I1)
- This reduces correlation risk — if one market has a bad day, the other is independent

### 5. Odds Threshold Matters
- A 92% probability pick at 1.11 (Charlton) gives +11% profit
- A 95% probability pick at 1.22 (Burgos) gives +22% profit
- **Double the profit for similar risk.** Always prioritize better odds when probability is similar.

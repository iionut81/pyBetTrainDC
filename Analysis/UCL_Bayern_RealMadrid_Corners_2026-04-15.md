## EVALUATED PICKS

### PICK 1: Under 7.5 Corners First Half @ 1.15

**Corner baseline:**
- First leg: 18 total corners → estimated 8-9 in first half alone
- Bayern home avg: 10.07 total → ~4.5 first half
- BUT: UCL knockout intensity >> league average
- Real needs to score → attacking from minute 1 → more corners early

**Game state analysis:**
- Real Madrid at 1-2 aggregate → MUST attack → wide play → corners
- Bayern at home, pressing high → forced corners from blocked attacks
- Both teams elite quality → sustained attacking pressure both directions
- Early goal risk HIGH → if Bayern score early, Real pushes even harder → corner spike

**Estimate first half corners: 6-8**
- Probability Under 7.5 FH: **~55-60%**
- Implied probability at 1.15: **87%**
- **Edge: -27% to -32% NEGATIVE**

**Score: 5/10 — PASS**

**Why it fails:** First leg data (18 total, ~9 FH estimate) directly contradicts this Under. Bayern pressing at home + Real attacking = corner festival from the start. Odds imply 87% but reality is closer to coin flip.

### PICK 3: Real Madrid Over 2.5 Corners @ 1.14 — RECOMMENDED

**Corner baseline:**
- Real Madrid UCL average: **6.56 corners/game** — more than double the 2.5 line
- First leg: Real won **7 corners** at Bernabeu (home)
- Away from home they still average 4-5 corners in UCL
- La Liga away games: avg 10.29 total (Real contributes ~4.5-5)

**Game state analysis (critical):**
- Real Madrid trails 1-2 on aggregate → **MUST attack**
- Real cannot park the bus — they need at least 1 goal, ideally 2
- Mbappe + Vinicius + Bellingham = elite wide attackers who generate corners
- Crosses, shots blocked, set pieces → all produce corners
- Even in a bad game, Real's quality generates 3+ corners through sheer attacking volume

**Scenario analysis:**
| Scenario | Real corners | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Real dominates, creates chances | 6-8 | 30% |
| Balanced game, both attack | 4-6 | 40% |
| Bayern dominates, Real counters | 3-4 | 20% |
| Real completely shut out | 1-2 | 10% |

**Weighted estimate: ~5.1 corners for Real Madrid**
- Probability Over 2.5: **~90-92%**
- Implied probability at 1.14: **87.7%**
- **Edge: +2.3% to +4.3% POSITIVE**

**Score: 9/10 — BET**

**Why it works:**
1. Real Madrid averages 6.56 corners/game in UCL — 2.6x the line
2. They got 7 corners in the first leg even without the aggregate pressure
3. Now they MUST attack (down 1-2) → even more attacking intent → more corners
4. Mbappe, Vinicius, Bellingham generate corners through wide play naturally
5. 2.5 is an extremely low threshold — Real gets 3+ in virtually every UCL game

**How I lose this bet:**
- Real falls behind 0-3 early and mentally collapses (extremely unlikely vs their quality)
- Bayern's press is so dominant that Real can't advance past midfield for 90 minutes
- Red card for Real Madrid early → 10 men, park the bus → fewer attacking corners
- All three scenarios combined probability: **~8-10%**

FINAL RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════

1. Never predict based only on H2H results.

2. Never rely only on the last 5 matches.

3. Always combine:
   - Long-term data
   - Recent form
   - Home/Away split
   - Tactical matchup
   - Coaching philosophy
   - Expected lineups
   - Motivation
   - Weather
   - Referee

4. Do not automatically assume that a strong team means a first-half goal.

5. Do not automatically assume that a team needing victory means Over 0.5 HT Goals.

6. A strong Over 0.5 HT profile should ideally be supported by at least two of the following:

   - Strong first-half scoring history
   - High attacking tempo
   - Tactical mismatch
   - Defensive vulnerability
   - Strong motivation to attack
   - High-quality expected lineups
   - High transition potential
   - High shot volume

7. If both teams have:

   - Low first-half goal rates
   - Conservative coaches
   - Low match urgency
   - Strong defensive lineups

   THEN:

   PASS

8. Always provide sources used for:

   - Team form
   - Expected lineups
   - Injuries
   - Suspensions
   - Coach information
   - Referee
   - Weather
   - Competition context

9. The final answer must clearly state:

   BET

   or

   PASS

   with a concise explanation of the main reason.

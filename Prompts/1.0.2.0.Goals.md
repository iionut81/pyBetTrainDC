🔴 STEP 1 — HARD DATA FILTER (BASELINE)
A. Combined Goal Environment

Calculate (din date verificate Step 0):

Team A GF/match + GA/match = combined A
Team B GF/match + GA/match = combined B
Average = (combined A + combined B) / 2

Rules:
Average > 3.6 → ❌ PASS
3.2–3.6 → ⚠️ Caution
2.6–3.2 → ✅ Good
<2.6 → 🔥 Premium

B. Combined xG Environment

Calculate (din fbref / sofascore):

Team A xG + xGA
Team B xG + xGA
Rules:
Combined xG > 3.4 → ❌ PASS
3.0–3.4 → ⚠️ Caution
2.4–3.0 → ✅ Good
<2.4 → 🔥 Premium

🆕 v2.2 REFINED RULES:
After detecting dead-rubber, check OFFENSIVE PROFILE:
- BOTH teams avg goals/match > 1.5 (offensive teams) → ❌ HARD PASS (chaos risk)
- ONE team avg goals/match > 1.5, OTHER < 1.5 → DOWNGRADE −2pp (mixed risk)
- BOTH teams avg goals/match < 1.5 (defensive teams) → DOWNGRADE −2pp ONLY (defensive nature persists)

WHY refined: Confirmed 01.05.2026 with two contrasting dead-rubber cases:
- Le Havre (1.5+ GF) vs Metz (1.5+ GF) 26.04.2026 → 4-4 (8 goals!) → U4.5 LOST ✅ confirms HARD PASS for offensive
- Andorra (low GF) vs Albacete (low GF) 01.05.2026 → 0-1 (1 goal) → U4.5 would have WON → soft downgrade was correct

Distinction:
- Mid-season mid-table → still want to climb table → disciplined
- Late-season mid-table safe + offensive teams → entertainment mode chaos
- Late-season mid-table safe + defensive teams → defensive reflex persists, low scoring stays

EXCEPTIONS (still BET if):
- One team chasing Europe/playoffs (motivation to defend exists)
- One team in title race (controlled match)
- Cup match (different motivation pattern)
- Top team in late season chasing trophy (discipline still there)

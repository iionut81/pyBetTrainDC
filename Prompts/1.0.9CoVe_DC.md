OBJECTIVE

Identify extremely low-risk Double Chance bets (1.20–1.40) where the selected team has minimal probability of losing.

Focus:
- defensive stability
- low volatility
- predictable match structure
- human factors (injuries, coach, motivation, schedule)

--------------------------------------------------

CRITICAL RULE — RESEARCH ADJUSTMENT CAP (added 2026-04-18)

After research, the research-adjusted probability must NOT exceed model probability by more than +3pp for DC bets.

If research pushes probability past +3pp (e.g., stacking "missing GK + missing defenders + weak attack + H2H"), STOP and ask:
"Why is the market priced lower than my research?"
→ Bookmakers price sharp. Large edge (>+7pp vs market implied) is usually a trap, not an opportunity.

Lesson cost: Udinese 1X @ 1.29 (market implied 77.5%) → research pushed to 88% (+10.5pp) → LOST 0-1 to Parma.
Even with Parma missing GK + 2 defenders + worst attack in Serie A + 3 Udinese clean sheets + H2H 3-0 Udinese.

Apply this cap BEFORE final scoring.

STEP 1 — NOT LOSING PROFILE (CRITICAL)

A. Loss Resistance (last 10 matches)

0–1 losses → AUTO QUALIFY
2 losses → acceptable
≥3 losses → PASS

B. Defensive Stability

Check:
- goals conceded per game
- xGA
- clean sheet frequency

Rules:
>1.5 conceded avg → PASS
1.2–1.5 → caution
<1.2 → strong
<1.0 → elite

C. Heavy Defeat Check

If team frequently loses by 2+ goals → PASS
If losses are rare and minimal → STRONG DC PROFILE

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 2 — MATCH TYPE FILTER

A. Ideal Matches

- strong home team with structure
- mid-table vs mid-table
- slight favorite (not huge gap)

B. Avoid Completely

- top 3 vs bottom 3 with motivation
- derby matches
- high-pressure games (title/relegation)
- inconsistent teams

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 — HUMAN FACTORS (CRUCIAL EDGE)

A. Injuries / Squad News

Positive:
- full defensive line available
- starting goalkeeper present

Negative (AUTO PASS):
- goalkeeper missing
- 2+ defenders missing

Bonus:
- opponent missing striker/playmaker → +2

--------------------------------------------------

B. Coach & Tactical Stability

Prefer:
- defensive or pragmatic coach
- structured team

Avoid:
- high pressing chaotic teams
- unstable tactical systems

--------------------------------------------------

C. Favorite Risk Detection

Check opponent:

- rotation expected → +2
- fatigue (European match) → +2
- low motivation → +2

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 — SCHEDULE & CONTEXT EDGE

Positive:
- opponent played midweek (Europe/cup)
- long travel
- rotation risk

Negative:
- opponent fully rested and motivated

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 5 — FINAL REALITY CHECK (REVIZUIT v1.3)

Ask the RIGHT question:

❌ DON'T ASK: "How does this pick lose?" (răspuns mereu = roșu/penalty/individual error → fals safe)

✅ ASK: "Dacă echipa mea NU ia roșu și NU primește penalty against, mai poate ADVERSARUL să câștige outright?"

--- DECISION TREE ---

If answer includes (NON-FREAK losing scenarios):
- clear domination by opponent
- big quality gap
- defensive weakness exposed
- opponent in hot form streak
- tactical mismatch (favored side has weak setup)
- key player out (striker, GK, anchor midfielder)
- motivation gap (must-win opponent vs comfortable favorite)

→ PASS

If losing requires COMBINATION of freak events:
- red card AND penalty against
- multiple individual errors stacked
- early red card (< 30min) for favored side

→ ACCEPT (freak combination ~3-5%, în margin-ul cotei)

If losing requires SINGLE freak event:
- red card alone (~15-20% rate per match)
- penalty alone (~25-30% rate per match)
- single individual error

→ NU AUTO-PASS, NU AUTO-ACCEPT
→ Asta e DISCIPLINE TRIGGER:
  • Reverify dacă ai ratat un scenariu real non-freak
  • Dacă găsești unul → revenire la PASS branch
  • Dacă tot nu găsești → ACCEPT (cota deja price-uiește freak risk în margin)

--- WHY THE NUANCE ---

Roșu și penalty NU sunt evenimente "freak":
- Carton roșu = ~15-20% din meciuri (1 din 5)
- Penalty = ~25-30% din meciuri (1 din 4)
- ≥1 freak event per match = ~40-45%

Acestea sunt evenimente regulate, NU black swan. Bookmakerul le price-uiește deja în margin (~5-10pp).

Dar NU orice roșu/penalty flipează DC-ul:
- Roșu LATE (>70min) după ce game-state e setat → DC-ul oricum hit
- Penalty pentru echipa ta → ajută DC
- Penalty against pentru echipă tare home → adesea îl recuperează

REAL freak combination care flipează DC:
- Roșu PLUS penalty against PLUS în primele 30min
- Probabilitate ~3-5% per meci → SAFE, în margin

Discipline check: dacă SINGURUL tău scenariu de pierdere e "freak event," te forțează să cauți un scenariu REAL. Dacă nu există, BET cu confidence.

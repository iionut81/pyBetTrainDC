CoVe — Football Double Chance (DC)
Version 1.1 — Low Odds Stability Model (1.20–1.40)

OBJECTIVE

Identify extremely low-risk Double Chance bets (1.20–1.40) where the selected team has minimal probability of losing.

Focus:
- defensive stability
- low volatility
- predictable match structure
- human factors (injuries, coach, motivation, schedule)

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — NOT LOSING PROFILE (CRITICAL)

A. Loss Resistance (last 10 matches)

0–1 losses → AUTO QUALIFY
2 losses → acceptable
≥3 losses → PASS

B. Defensive Stability

Check:
- goals conceded per game
- xGA
- clean sheet frequency

Rules:
>1.5 conceded avg → PASS
1.2–1.5 → caution
<1.2 → strong
<1.0 → elite

C. Heavy Defeat Check

If team frequently loses by 2+ goals → PASS
If losses are rare and minimal → STRONG DC PROFILE

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 2 — MATCH TYPE FILTER

A. Ideal Matches

- strong home team with structure
- mid-table vs mid-table
- slight favorite (not huge gap)

B. Avoid Completely

- top 3 vs bottom 3 with motivation
- derby matches
- high-pressure games (title/relegation)
- inconsistent teams

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 — HUMAN FACTORS (CRUCIAL EDGE)

A. Injuries / Squad News

Positive:
- full defensive line available
- starting goalkeeper present

Negative (AUTO PASS):
- goalkeeper missing
- 2+ defenders missing

Bonus:
- opponent missing striker/playmaker → +2

--------------------------------------------------

B. Coach & Tactical Stability

Prefer:
- defensive or pragmatic coach
- structured team

Avoid:
- high pressing chaotic teams
- unstable tactical systems

--------------------------------------------------

C. Favorite Risk Detection

Check opponent:

- rotation expected → +2
- fatigue (European match) → +2
- low motivation → +2

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 — SCHEDULE & CONTEXT EDGE

Positive:
- opponent played midweek (Europe/cup)
- long travel
- rotation risk

Negative:
- opponent fully rested and motivated

--------------------------------------------------

STEP 5 — FINAL REALITY CHECK

Ask:

“How does this pick realistically lose?”

If answer includes:
- clear domination by opponent
- big quality gap
- defensive weakness

→ PASS

If losing scenario requires:
- red card
- penalty
- individual error

→ ACCEPT

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL SCORE (/10)

Loss resistance = /3
Defensive stability = /2
Match type = /2
Human factors = /2
Schedule/context = /1

--------------------------------------------------

RESULT INTERPRETATION

9–10 → PREMIUM (very high safety)
8 → STRONG
7 → borderline (only if needed)
<7 → PASS

--------------------------------------------------

OUTPUT FORMAT

Pick | Odds | Score | Confidence | Why It’s Safe | Risk

Example:
Team A 1X | 1.30 | 9/10 | Very High | Strong defense, opponent rotated | Red card + penalty

--------------------------------------------------

MANDATORY SEARCHES

“[Team A] vs [Team B] predicted lineups injuries”
“[Team A] missing players today”
“[Team B] rotation news”
“[League] latest team news”
“[Coach] pre match comments”

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL RULE

If ANY major uncertainty exists → PASS

Goal is NOT to find bets.
Goal is to eliminate losing scenarios.
